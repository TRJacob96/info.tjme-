What is this?

This is TRJacob's personal hub — basically everywhere my stuff lives, in one place, instead of scattered across random links.

my stuff

Links out to my GitHub, YouTube, and (eventually) Discord.

tools

Little offline utilities I built: a global-metadata.dat string editor, a general file editor, a code obfuscator, a browser fingerprint checker (InfoDrop), and Lookit — a Roblox/Fortnite/Rocket League stat lookup.

scripts

Bookmarklets and mod menus — EpicExplorer (a mobile web inspector) and Eclair.

my games

Games I've made or ported: Pixel League, a blocky Rocket League-style game, and Twin Tag, a Gorilla Tag–inspired copy.

files

Downloadable stuff, like TRNative — a userscript for playing Play Origin on mobile.

settings

The gear icon up top — toggle the camera zoom effect and the background music.

Everything here runs entirely in your browser — nothing you do on this site gets sent to me or stored anywhere, unless a specific tool says otherwise.
